Step-by-Step Guided Deduction
1
Analyze Linear Mass Densities Since the pulse travels from heavy to light string: μ₁ > μ₂.
2
Compare Phase Velocities Inverse square root relation v = √(T/μ) implies that v₂ > v₁ in the lighter string.
3
Determine Sign of Reflection Coefficient R Because v₂ > v₁, the numerator (v₂ − v₁) > 0, which makes R > 0.
